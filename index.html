HOW IT WORKS

Certificate Verification Process

CertChain verifies certificates through a secure and simple four-step process.

1

Issue Certificate

The college administrator issues or uploads a student certificate.

2

Generate Hash

The system generates a unique SHA-256 hash from the certificate file.

3

Store on Blockchain

The generated hash is securely stored inside the blockchain record.

4

Verify Certificate

The uploaded certificate hash is compared with the stored blockchain hash.

ABOUT CERTCHAIN

Prevent Fake and Modified Certificates

Traditional certificate verification can be slow, manual and vulnerable to fraud. CertChain provides a secure digital platform where certificate hashes are stored in blockchain records.

When a company or university uploads a certificate, the system generates a new hash and compares it with the original stored hash. Any modification immediately produces a different verification result.
